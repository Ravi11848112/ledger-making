Privacy Policy for Ledger Maker

Google API Disclosure: Ledger Maker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Collection & Usage: Ledger Maker accesses your Google Sheets account solely to export transaction logs, collection records, and balance reports created within the app. We do not store, sell, or share your Google private data or spreadsheet contents with third parties.

Data Retention & Deletion: Users can disconnect Google Sheets integration at any time via settings or revoke access through Google Account permissions.
